Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form opens a prepared email in Gmail. After you press Send, the cafe will reply to confirm availability, options, and pricing.

What can I ask Casa Juana about?

You can inquire about cafe table reservations, catering and packed meals, private events, pickleball court bookings, pool day-use, and overnight stays.

Where can I see the menu and prices?

Menu choices and prices can vary by order. Email or message the cafe on Facebook for current options and a quote.

When is the cafe open?

The cafe is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. For event and overnight inquiries, you can message the cafe anytime.
